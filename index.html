Personal Task Sync

個人用の小さなツールです。自分の PC で毎朝、自分宛てのメールから締切や返信が必要な件を拾い、自分の Google Tasks のリストに追加します。

A small personal tool. Every morning it runs on the owner's own PC, picks up deadlines and replies needed from the owner's mail, and adds them to the owner's own Google Tasks list.
